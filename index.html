ℹ️ About MyFeed

✨ What does MyFeed do?

MyFeed recommends posts based on your interests and interactions. The system infers your preferences from the content you engage with to personalise future recommendations.

Liking multiple posts from the same category increases your interest score for that category, allowing MyFeed to recommend more similar content.

You can also explore why posts are recommended, provide feedback using Show more or Show less, and adjust your interests directly in Settings.

🎯 How well can it do it?

MyFeed uses a simplified recommendation system based on interest scores and user interactions. It can adapt recommendations to your preferences, but it cannot always determine whether a post accurately reflects your interests.

Recommendations are limited to four content categories: photography, cooking, fitness and travel. MyFeed does not analyse watch time, searches or other browsing behaviour.
